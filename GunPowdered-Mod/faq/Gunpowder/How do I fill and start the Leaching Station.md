How do I fill and start the Leaching Station?

Use it (E): it takes half of its full batch of Bat Guano, Water and Wood from your inventory, or as much as you have (5 + 5 + 2 without upgrades), and gives you all the Saltpeter waiting in it. You can fill it at any time, also while it is working, up to the amount it can hold. There is no start button: as soon as it holds at least 10 Bat Guano + 10 Water + 4 Wood it starts by itself, and it never starts with less; the hover text says exactly what is missing. A run uses only whole 10 + 10 + 4 portions (each gives 5 Saltpeter, up to the station's full batch); the rest stays in. The finished Saltpeter piles up in the cauldron and the next run starts at once while a whole portion is still inside; with less than that the station stops. Sleeping skips time for the station too.

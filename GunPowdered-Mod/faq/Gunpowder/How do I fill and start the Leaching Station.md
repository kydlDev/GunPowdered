How do I fill and start the Leaching Station?

Use it (E): it takes half of its full batch of Bat Guano, Water and Wood from your inventory, or as much as you have (5 + 5 + 2 without upgrades). It starts by itself once it holds a full batch. To start earlier, press Shift + E, or E when you have nothing more to put in. It needs at least 10 Bat Guano + 10 Water + 4 Wood; the hover text says exactly what is missing. A run uses only whole 10 + 10 + 4 portions (each gives 5 Saltpeter); the rest stays in for the next run. Sleeping skips time for the station too.

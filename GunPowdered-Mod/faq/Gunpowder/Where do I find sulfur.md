Where do I find sulfur?

- Sulfurous Mud: yellow mud around swamp geysers and in the water within 15 m of Draugr bone piles (not in Sunken Crypts; a pile with no water around has none). A pickaxe gives 1-3 Sulfur; it fills up again 2 in-game days after it is emptied.
- Blobs and Oozers: 10 % chance to drop 1-2 Sulfur when killed.

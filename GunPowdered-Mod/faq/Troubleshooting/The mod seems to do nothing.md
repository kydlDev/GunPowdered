The mod seems to do nothing, what do I check?

Check that KydlCore is installed and that [General] Enable Mod is true in BepInEx/config/com.kydldev.GunPowdered.cfg. Each part has its own Enable switch under [General] (Guano, Water, Saltpeter, Sulfur, Gunpowder, Grenades, Cannons, Musket, Explosive Arrows, Dynamite). Build pieces are in the hammer's KY-Items category. Still stuck: turn on [DebugLog] Enable, try again and send BepInEx/LogOutput.log on the KydlDev Discord.

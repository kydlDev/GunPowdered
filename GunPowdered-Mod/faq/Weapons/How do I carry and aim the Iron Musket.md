How do I carry and aim the Iron Musket?

When you run, the musket goes down to your right side, held under the stock. It stays there until you click the mouse wheel ([Musket] Aim Key): it comes up to the shoulder and can fire again. It fires only when raised.

![Musket](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/kydlDev/GunPowdered/main/GunPowdered-Mod/images/Musket.gif)

While it hangs at your side your left hand is free for a Torch or a Shield (the shield blocks). Other items, like a club, put the musket away. Raising the musket puts the torch or shield away.
You can reload while running. A dodge roll keeps the musket in your right hand and does not lower an aimed musket.
The musket blocks like a light weapon: block 10 (15 at Blocking 100), parry bonus 1.5, and it does not slow you down.

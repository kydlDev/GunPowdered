Which weapons damage buildings, rocks and trees?

- Spiked Grenade and Explosive Arrow: damage buildings, trees and rocks in their blast.
- Hand Cannon: buildings and rocks take the ball's full damage and stop it; it passes through one tree or creature and loses half its speed and damage.
- Mobile Cannon: like the Hand Cannon, with more pickaxe damage; breaks rock and ore up to tool tier 5.
- Dynamite: made for digging. It breaks buildings, trees, rocks and ore up to tool tier 2 (iron level) and leaves a round crater, but hurts creatures only a fifth as much.
- Deep North Dynamite Pack: breaks rock up to tool tier 6, including the frozen gammel trolls of the Deep North, in a 20 m blast with a 5 m crater.
The Iron Musket only hurts creatures.

How do I use dynamite?

Hold it and press attack to set it down: your character kneels and stands up again, and the placed stick sparkles light red until it is lit, so you can find it. Then use it (E) with a Torch in your hand to light the fuse; it blows after 5 seconds (3 seconds with the Explosives skill at 100). The 4 m blast breaks rock, ore, trees and buildings and leaves a round crater. Creatures take only a fifth of the damage, so it is a mining tool, not a weapon.

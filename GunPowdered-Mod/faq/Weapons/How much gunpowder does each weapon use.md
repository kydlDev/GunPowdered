How much gunpowder does each weapon use?

- Cartridge: 1 per cartridge (1 shot).
- Spiked Grenade: 3 per craft of 5.
- Explosive Arrow: 2 per craft of 10.
- Dynamite: 5 per craft of 5 (1 per stick).
- Hand Cannon: 1 per shot, loaded with the ball.
- Mobile Cannon: 3 per shot, loaded with the ball.
- Deep North Dynamite Pack: no extra powder; it is made of 4 Dynamite and 2 Spiked Grenades.
Cannon Balls can be picked up and used again after the shot.

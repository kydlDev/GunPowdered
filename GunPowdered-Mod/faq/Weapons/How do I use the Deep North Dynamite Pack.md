How do I use the Deep North Dynamite Pack?

Set it down like a stick of dynamite, then use it with a Torch in your hand to light the 4 second fuse, and run. Every player within 30 m sees the countdown on screen. The blast reaches 20 m: it breaks rock up to tool tier 6, such as the frozen gammel trolls of the Deep North, wrecks buildings and trees and leaves a crater 5 m wide and 2 m deep. Stay well outside 20 m.

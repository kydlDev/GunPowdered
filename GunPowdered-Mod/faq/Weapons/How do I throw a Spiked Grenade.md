How do I throw a Spiked Grenade?

Hold the attack button to aim: an arc and a ring show where it lands. Release to throw, or block to cancel. The grenade lands, its fuse burns for 3 s (1 s with the Explosives skill at 100), then it bursts in a 4 m blast that hurts creatures and damages buildings, trees and rocks. Step away from where it lands.

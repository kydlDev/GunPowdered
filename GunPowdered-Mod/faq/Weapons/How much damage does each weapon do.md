How much damage does each weapon do?

Default numbers per hit or blast:
- Iron Musket: 50 pierce per bullet, plus burning (5 fire per second for 5 s).
- Spiked Grenade: 50 blunt + 30 pierce in a 4 m blast, plus burning (5 per second for 5 s).
- Explosive Arrow: the arrow's 30 pierce, then a 3 m burst of 20 blunt + 5 fire.
- Hand Cannon: 70 blunt + 30 pierce + 150 pickaxe per ball, plus burning (10 per second for 5 s). After passing through a tree or creature the ball keeps half its speed and damage.
- Mobile Cannon: 120 blunt + 50 pierce + 250 pickaxe per ball, plus burning (10 per second for 5 s).
- Dynamite: 100 blunt, chop and pickaxe in a 4 m blast. Creatures take a fifth (20) plus burning (5 per second for 5 s).
- Deep North Dynamite Pack: 400 blunt and chop + 500 pickaxe in a 20 m blast. Creatures take a fifth (80) plus burning (30 per second for 5 s).

The creature's resistances and the game's backstab bonus still apply.
